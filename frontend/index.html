Retrieval & Model Infrastructure

  • Dense Vector Store: Qdrant HNSW ANN with 28,541 vectors
  • Lexical Search: Inverted BM25 index with 48,206 documents
  • Embedding Model: intfloat/multilingual-e5-small (384-dim)
  • Fusion Strategy: Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF k=60)
  • Reranker: cross-encoder/mmarco-mMiniLMv2-L12-H384-v1
  • Generator: Sarvam AI 105B LLM (sarvam-105b)
  • STT & TTS: Saarika v2.5 (saarika:v2.5) & Bulbul v2 (bulbul:v2)
